About me

Technical engineering experience with project-management responsibility.

I am an Automotive Project Manager and Manufacturing Engineer with more than 12 years of experience in Body-in-White (BIW) manufacturing, robotic simulation, process planning, manufacturing engineering and project coordination.

My experience covers the engineering and manufacturing lifecycle, from process development and robotic simulation through validation, production readiness and project execution.

In my current role, I coordinate automotive manufacturing and BIW projects across engineering, manufacturing, quality, suppliers and customers. My responsibilities include project planning, timeline and deliverable management, change coordination, technical problem solving, documentation and customer communication.

I also bring hands-on expertise in robotic simulation, process optimization, tooling and fixture validation, robot reachability, collision analysis, MRS and OLP.
